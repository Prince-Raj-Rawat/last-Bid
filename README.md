# Last-Bid 🎨

*A story-driven drawing game where every painting could bring you closer to saving a life.*

## About the Game

**Last-Bid** is a browser-based drawing and bidding game built using HTML.

You play as an artist who needs to earn enough money to afford an operation for your sick and dying mother. Your paintings become your means of survival: draw, present your artwork to customers, and earn money through their bids.

Every customer and every amount earned can affect your journey. Your decisions help determine how the story concludes, with **six possible endings — three good and three bad**.

Will your art earn enough to save your mother?

## Gameplay

The core gameplay revolves around three activities:

- **Draw:** Create artwork using freehand drawing with your mouse.
- **Bid and Earn:** Sell your drawings through customer bidding to earn money.
- **Shape Your Ending:** The customers you sell to and the money you earn influence which ending you reach.
